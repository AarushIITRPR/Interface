# Meridian portal design system

## North star

Meridian is a calm institutional research instrument. The approved archive defines its composition: warm paper, blue ink, terracotta editorial emphasis, square ruled surfaces, and a clear separation between human workflow and engineering setup.

## Information hierarchy

Instructor primary views contain only what is needed to understand the session, act on the protocol, recognize recording problems, communicate with the subject, and review evidence. Study overview visualizes genuine live sensor input and its connection, transmission, and quality health. The session ledger remains in Recording review. Technical acquisition details remain available in Workspace settings.

Subject views contain only the current task and its direct controls. The subject never sees sensor status, expected emotion, internal workflow state, or operator diagnostics.

## Typography

- Display: Georgia, regular, tight editorial spacing
- Operational: Arial or Helvetica, compact bold labels
- Body copy: readable, short, and subordinate
- Controls: direct verbs with a visible 44px target where space permits

## Color

- Paper: `#f1ece2`
- Light paper: `#f9f5ee`
- Ink: `#1c2936`
- Muted: `#68727c`
- Terracotta: `#ad503c`
- Ready: `#1d7f60`

## Interaction

- Motion lasts about 180ms and communicates state continuity only.
- Focus is visible on every interactive element.
- Healthy state is visually quiet. Required attention uses terracotta or red plus text.
- Advanced acquisition setup uses modal progressive disclosure.
- Interface copy contains no em dashes.
